programdemos/activations/activations.xtl

Activation functions, graphed: every function of this repository's Activations library and its derivative over x from -6 to 6, two charts a family (the functions, their slopes), drawn by X_eTaL-libraries' Plot with an x axis of real values (p:f_unctionChart!). The live page adds sliders for the parameters and an editor for an activation of your own.
