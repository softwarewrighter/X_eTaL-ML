programlibs/Learn/demos/blobs.xtl

Learn's four models on one set of points: three blobs of 20 (around 0 0, 6 6 and 0 6, a fixed ripple instead of chance). k-means finds them without their labels; kNN and logistic regression learn them from their labels; PCA finds the direction they spread along most. Run with "just demo-lib Learn blobs".
