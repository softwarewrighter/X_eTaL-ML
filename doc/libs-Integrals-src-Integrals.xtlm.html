macro librarylibs/Integrals/src/Integrals.xtlm

Integrals: a definite integral written as a paper prints it, computed by a quadrature rule -- "integral tau:=a..b by trapezoid 64 | body"

Import it with an alias of your choice: "integ:" u_se< "Integrals". Put libs/Integrals/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Integrals/docs. Names with m: are macros; those under h: are private to this file.

A macro is a function from the source text written left and right of its call to the source that replaces the call, before the program is compiled (X_eTaL MC10); xetal expand FILE shows the result. The call is X_eTaL's Saga 42 shape (its decisions 2 to 4, 2026-10-17): integral read like sum, the rule and the grid as option words with a documented default, improper integrals refused. Its reader uses all of its text or refuses it. Decision 1 puts integral in X_eTaL-libraries' Equations in the end; this library is the same call standing alone, lifted whole when that lands (the body here is X_eTaL, there the equation grammar).

ᵐi̲ntegral< : Unit -> Char -> Char

macro · line 36

The value of a definite integral by a quadrature rule: "integral tau:=a..b by trapezoid 64 | body". The variable and its bounds (any X_eTaL giving a Float; a whole-number literal is made Float), then the rule (trapezoid or simpson) and the grid (how many steps; simpson needs an even number), then | and the body: X_eTaL over the variable, which the macro binds to the grid's points as one vector, a, a + h, ..., b, so the body is evaluated once for the whole grid. Without "by" the rule is trapezoid 64. A bound of inf is refused: write a truncation (0..20) and show the tail is small, or change variables. Trapezoid is exact on lines, simpson on cubics; at a jump of the body the sample takes the body's value at that point as written.
