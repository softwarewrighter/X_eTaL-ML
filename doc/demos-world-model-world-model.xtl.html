programdemos/world-model/world-model.xtl

A tiny world model: a network that has watched a ball bounce in a box predicts the next frame from the last three. The world is a 2 x 2 ball in a 12 x 12 box, moving 1 or 2 cells a step on each axis and bouncing off the walls: 1,936 states in all. The network (432 pixels in, 64 ReLU units, 144 pixels out) was trained offline on four fifths of them (train/); the other fifth it never saw. Here it predicts the next frame of every state at once, as one batch, then dreams: fed its own predictions, it plays the ball forward on its own.
