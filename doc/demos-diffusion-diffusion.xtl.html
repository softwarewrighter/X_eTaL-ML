programdemos/diffusion/diffusion.xtl

A learned denoiser: pictures from noise, step by step. The pictures are 10 x 10, one of three shapes (a disk, a hollow square, a cross) at some place, a pixel -1 off or 1 on. A small convolutional network (train/) was trained to see the clean picture in a noisy one at ten levels of noise. Sampling runs it backward: from a picture of pure noise, each step predicts the clean picture, infers the noise that must have been added, and steps down one level (DDIM, no new noise), so the picture that comes out depends only on the noise that went in.
