librarylibs/LoRA/src/LoRA.xtl

LoRA: low-rank adapters: the adapted forward pass in factored form, the merge, the counts, the gradients and steps, LoRA-FA

Import it with an alias of your choice: "lo:" u_se< "LoRA". Put libs/LoRA/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/LoRA/docs. Names with l: are exported; h: names are private to this file.

A frozen weight matrix W (m x n: m outputs, n inputs) is adapted by a low-rank update s B A: A is r x n, B is m x r, the rank r much smaller than m and n, s the scale alpha / r. An adapter is the tuple (W, A, B, s). The inputs are columns: x is one input of n numbers or an n x N matrix of N inputs, and W x is W '+ '× i̲nner x. The forward pass keeps the factors apart, B (A x), as training does: the merged matrix W + s B A is made only to deploy it.

Training moves A and B by the gradients of the squared error (g_rads, s_gd, a_dam through the Optim library); LoRA-FA freezes A (regenerated from a seed, s_eeded) and trains B alone; QLoRA keeps W in 4 bits (NF4, through the Quant library) and computes with it dequantized.
