programdemos/micro-bert/micro-bert.xtl

A tiny BERT in X_eTaL: the encoder half of a transformer (Devlin et al. 2019), 2 layers, 16 numbers wide, 2 attention heads, 7,594 weights, trained offline to fill a blank letter in baby names. Every letter sees every other, before it and after it, which a GPT's causal mask forbids. One encoder layer is a pipeline (Flow), applied L times by p_ower; the forward pass gives the same scores as the Rust model it was trained in, and the same as the layer written out, and the attention as Equations writes it.
