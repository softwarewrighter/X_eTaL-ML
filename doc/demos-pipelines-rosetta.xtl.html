programdemos/pipelines/rosetta.xtl

One CNN layer four ways, a Rosetta Stone: as a paper prints it (an equation, read by X_eTaL-libraries' Equations), as a pipeline (Flow), and as plain X_eTaL (this repository's Conv). The pipeline's expansion, the fourth way, is what xetal expand rosetta.xtl shows. All give the same feature maps.
