programdemos/moe-finetune/moe-finetune.xtl

Fine-tuning a tiny mixture-of-experts, each method in turn. The base (base.xtl, trained by train/base.xtl) classifies tokens of 4 kinds, each by its kind's own rule; the new task changes kind 3's rule. A method fine-tunes the base on 200 tokens of the new task (alone, or mixed with 200 of the old task's: replay), the base's own weights frozen, with LoRA adapters (rank 2) on some of its parts: the shared layer only; every expert, the router frozen; both; the two experts the new tokens use most; every expert with the router trained too; or a new expert, added with its own router row, trained alone. Each is measured by the new task's accuracy, the old task's (the kinds whose rule did not change: what fine-tuning must not break, 3 points the bar), the numbers it trains and their bytes, where the tokens are routed before and after, and how large each expert's update is. Then four adapters compared (shared LoRA, expert LoRA, LoRA-FA, shared VeRA), and the adapters deployed: kept apart against merged (LoRA exactly, QLoRA not), two tasks' adapters switched on one base, and an expert cache measuring what each way moves. Last, preference tuning: SFT on either of two valid answers, then DPO on pairs of them, the better preferred.
