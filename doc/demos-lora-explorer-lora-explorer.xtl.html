programdemos/lora-explorer/lora-explorer.xtl

LoRA explorer: one 8 x 8 projection adapted by a rank-r update. The frozen W, the trainable A (r x 8) and B (8 x r); the adapted layer y = W x + s B (A x) as a pipeline of equations, drawn as math, its factors kept apart; the update s B A, its rank and the merge; what adapters cost on a large mixture-of-experts model; a backward step, stage by stage; LoRA and LoRA-FA trained side by side to fit a known update, against the best any rank-r update can do (the truncated SVD); W in 4 bits, uniform and NF4 (QLoRA's base); then the rank sweep.
