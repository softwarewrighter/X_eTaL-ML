X_eTaL ML: a timeline of machine learning

The key papers and milestones from 1943 to today, beside the demos here that show their ideas live. Years with more events are wider; quiet runs of years fold into one break.

Move with the buttons, the year box (1989, 2017-06 or 1990s), the minimap (click or drag), the wheel, or Home, End, Page Up and Down and the arrow keys. The address follows the view, so a year can be linked.
