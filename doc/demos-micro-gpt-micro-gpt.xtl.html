programdemos/micro-gpt/micro-gpt.xtl

A tiny GPT in X_eTaL: Karpathy's microgpt (1 layer, 16 numbers wide, 4 attention heads, 4,192 weights), trained offline on 32,033 baby names, inventing new ones a letter at a time. The forward pass reads a whole name at once under a causal mask, on the Norm, Attention and Sample libraries; it gives the same scores as the Rust model it was trained in.
