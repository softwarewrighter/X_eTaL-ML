programdemos/k-means/k-means.xtl

k-means, step by step: 300 points in five blobs, five centers. Each step gives every point the nearest center, then moves each center to the mean of its points (the Learn library's k_step: a few array expressions over all the points at once). From a poor start, five points of one blob, the centers travel across the plane to the blobs; from Learn's farthest-first start they barely need to.
