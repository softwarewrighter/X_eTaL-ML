programdemos/net-macro/train-it.xtl

Training a network from its one-line spec: the Net macro library writes, from "2 16 relu 16 relu 3 softmax", the network's backward pass and an Adam step (net:t_rain<) and the starting state (net:s_tate<); p_ower repeats the step. The task is net-macro's: which of three spiral arms is a point on? Here the 300 training points are made in X_eTaL, and the weights start small and random.
