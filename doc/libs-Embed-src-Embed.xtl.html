librarylibs/Embed/src/Embed.xtl

Embed: word embeddings from counts: co-occurrence, PPMI, a fixed projection, cosine neighbors

Import it with an alias of your choice: "em:" u_se< "Embed". Put libs/Embed/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Embed/docs. Names with l: are exported; h: names are private to this file.

A corpus is a vector of word numbers (from 1), each sentence ended by a 0. A word's embedding is a row: which words occur near it, weighted by how much more often than chance (PPMI), then projected to fewer dimensions. Words used alike get rows that point alike.
