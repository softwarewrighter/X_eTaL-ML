(docs/plan.md Saga 7 row 1; arXiv 2611.05344 Section 12) A live demo of the paper's hand-built in-context-learning circuit on the Attention library: a residual stream of named blocks (class, label, position, token type, duplicate flag, gate, slot key, query code), writers, a duplicate detector, a pooling head and readers with a sink; prompts picked or typed; knock out the writers, the duplicate detector or the gate, repeat a label or not; the readers' attention to the matching slots and to the sink, and the answer's margin; tests against the paper's numbers (the read law exact; the sink's share and the margins it reports).
