programdemos/icl-trained/icl-trained.xtl

In-context learning, trained: a small transformer (4 layers, 64 wide, 4 heads, a SwiGLU feed-forward layer of 128; 180,288 weights) trained from scratch on Gao and Gao's synthetic task (arXiv 2611.05344, Section 12 and its appendix) by train/, then taken apart here as the paper's authors took theirs apart (their toy/analyze_toy.py): the query's margin on unique-label and recurring-label prompts; the reader heads, by class-flip attribution; the slot swap; the gate, a direction at the query's arrow, decoded and steered. A prompt is the start token, demonstrations w w w → label, then the query w w w →: 48 words, each in two of eight classes, a sentence three words sharing one class, 32 labels redrawn for every prompt. Token numbers: 1 the start, 2 the arrow, 3 a line's end, 4 to 51 the words, 52 to 83 the labels.
