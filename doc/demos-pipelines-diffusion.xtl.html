programdemos/pipelines/diffusion.xtl

Diffusion sampling four ways, a second Rosetta Stone: the two equations of a latent diffusion sampler as a paper prints them, read by X_eTaL-libraries' Equations; the sampler as a pipeline (Flow); and in plain X_eTaL, as this repository's diffusion demo runs it. The pipeline's expansion, the fourth way, is what xetal expand diffusion.xtl shows. All give the same pictures.

The paper's two forms (bold z a latent, D the decoder, eps_theta the denoiser conditioned on the text embedding of the prompt): X0 = D(zT + sum_{t=T}^{1} [a_t z_t + b_t eps_theta(z_t, t, E_text(prompt)) + sigma_t eps_rand] - z_t) X0 = D(z0) = D(zT + sum_{t=1}^{T} dz_t) The first sum is a recurrence (its term at t uses the z_t the term before made), so it is one step as the paper's summand, applied T times; the second is a plain sum of the steps' differences, and telescopes to the same z0.
