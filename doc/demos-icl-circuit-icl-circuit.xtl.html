programdemos/icl-circuit/icl-circuit.xtl

A circuit for in-context learning, built by hand (Gao and Gao, arXiv 2611.05344, Section 12; reimplemented from its toy/toy_circuit.py, MIT). A prompt shows demonstrations, a sentence of some class and then a label, and asks for the label of a query sentence. Three attention layers answer it without a weight trained: writers stamp each label with its sentence's class (the slot's key), a duplicate detector marks a label seen before, a pooling head gathers those marks into a gate at the query, and readers match the query's class against the slot keys and copy the label. The readers' one other key is a sink at position 0: with no repeated label the gate stays closed and the readers rest there; a repeat opens it.
