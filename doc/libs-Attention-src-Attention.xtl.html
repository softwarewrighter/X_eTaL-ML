librarylibs/Attention/src/Attention.xtl

Attention: scaled dot-product scores, masked softmax weights, causal masks, a head

Import it with an alias of your choice: "at:" u_se< "Attention". Put libs/Attention/src on XETAL_PATH ("just path"); the reference is libs/Attention/docs. Names with l: are exported; h: names are private to this file.

Tokens X are a matrix, one token per row. A head's projections are matrices with a row per feature of X: Wq and Wk give queries and keys of d numbers each, Wv the values. A mask is n x n: 1 where a token may look at another, 0 where not. Attention is three array expressions: S = Q K^T / sqrt d, A = softmax of each row of S, Y = A V.
